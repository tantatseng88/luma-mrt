Luma to MRT

Load your next Luma event, pick the MRT station you're starting from, and get the lines, interchanges and stop to alight at. Nothing leaves this page.

Install the app on your phone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button in the toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮ at the top right.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen), then Install.

It opens full screen from your home screen and works without a connection.

About this app

App last updated
Event list last updated

Luma to MRT shows upcoming public Luma events in Singapore on a calendar. Pick an event and the MRT station you're starting from, and it gives you the lines, interchanges, the stop to get off at, the walk to the venue and when to leave.

Where event data comes from

SourceStatusNotes
Luma Singapore list (luma.com/discover/singapore)In usePublic events, no login. Fetched every 3 hours. Luma hides some addresses in this list until you register.
My registration (.ics file)In useAdds the address of an event you registered for. Saved on your device only.
Event link / type it inIn useFor events not in the Singapore list.
Luma iCal subscriptionPlannedWould add all your registrations automatically. Needs a small proxy server, and the private link must stay on your device.
Luma APINot usedFor event hosts only: needs Luma Plus and a key for a calendar you manage. It can't list events you're attending.

How it works

  1. A GitHub Action fetches Luma's Singapore events every 3 hours and publishes them with the app on GitHub Pages. Luma blocks browsers from reading the list directly.
  2. The app loads that list when it opens and when you tap Refresh events.
  3. Each event's map location gives the nearest MRT stations. A registered address replaces the approximate area.
  4. The route is worked out on the MRT network built into the app: about 2.3 minutes per stop and 5 minutes per change of line.
  5. Walking time is the straight-line distance plus 30% for streets, at 80 m a minute.
  6. The app works offline once opened, and tells you when a new version is available.

What's next

  • Suggest the starting station from your phone's location, only after you allow it.
  • Walking routes and the best station exit from OneMap.
  • Train disruption alerts from LTA DataMall, shown above the route.
  • Connect your Luma iCal subscription so registered addresses fill in automatically.
  • A reminder at your "leave by" time.

Limits

  • Covers the six MRT lines. No LRT, and Circle Line Stage 6 isn't included yet.
  • Travel and walking times are estimates. Check the map links before you go.
  • Luma's event list isn't an official API and could change without notice.

Station locations: sgraildata (open data). Events: Luma. Maps: Google Maps and OneMap.
